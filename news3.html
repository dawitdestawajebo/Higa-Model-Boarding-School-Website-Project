Preparations Completed to Resume Suspended Dormitory Construction at Higa Model Boarding School

The Kembata People's Development Association held a consultative meeting with the teachers and administrative staff of Higa Model Boarding School to resume the halted construction of the student dormitory building. The discussions focused on defining collaboration strategies and resource mobilization plans to bring the project to completion.

Key highlights from the meeting include:

  • Infrastructure Goals: As Higa Model Boarding School serves as a center of excellence in the zone, creating a conducive environment for students is a top priority. Stakeholders agreed to complete the dormitory, construct a main entrance gate, and carry out campus beautification projects by mobilizing local natives and key partners..
  • Staff Commitment: Teachers and administrative staff reviewed the project design and pledged support through labor, expertise, financial contributions, and materials. They also committed to leading community outreach and fundraising initiatives.
  • Collaborative Action: The session concluded with a joint agreement to execute all infrastructure upgrades swiftly, with the Kembata People's Development Association reaffirming its commitment to coordinate closely with stakeholders and the community to see the project through.
