Why Donate?

Your donation makes a real difference! Higa Model Boarding School provides quality education to talented students from underprivileged backgrounds. Many students face financial challenges that limit their access to resources. Your support helps cover tuition, learning materials, and living expenses—ensuring they can focus on their studies without worry. By donating, you empower the next generation of leaders to build a brighter future for their communities.

Higa Model Boarding School

Higa Model Boarding School is a project of Gogota Care, a non-profit organization. We are dedicated to offering high-caliber education and empowering model secondary students in Central Ethiopia.
