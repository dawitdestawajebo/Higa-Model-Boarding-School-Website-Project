DISCOVER OUR SCHOOL

Higa Model Boarding School is a leading secondary school in Kembata Zone, Ethiopia, established to provide high-quality education and create opportunities for academically talented students. It is recognized as one of the high-performing schools in Ethiopia, with a strong reputation for academic excellence and student achievement.

The school welcomes high-achieving students from different parts of the region, selected based on their performance in the Grade 8 Ministry examination and entrance tests. This brings together talented students from diverse backgrounds, creating a competitive and inspiring learning environment.

We provide a supportive boarding environment where students can focus on education, skill building, and career preparation. With an emphasis on academic excellence, discipline, and community values, Higa Model Boarding School nurtures capable future leaders.

Our Achievements

Academic Achievement

Higa Model Boarding School has once again demonstrated its commitment to academic excellence through the outstanding performance of its Grade 12 students in the 2017 E.C. Ethiopian University Entrance Examination. All Grade 12 students successfully passed the national university entrance examination. Among them, 16 students scored 500 or higher out of 600, demonstrating exceptional academic achievement.

Recognition for Academic Excellence

In 2017 E.C., Higa Model Boarding School was honored by the Central Ethiopia Regional State in recognition of its outstanding academic performance and achievement. The school was awarded a trophy and certificate of recognition for being among the high-achieving schools in the regional state.
